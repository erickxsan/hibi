Privacy policy

Effective July 12, 2026

hibi is a class-management app for independent teachers. This policy explains what information the service uses and why.

Information hibi processes

  • Google account information: your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Class records you enter: students, guardian contacts, phone numbers, groups, grades, attendance, class logs, payment records, notes, and preferences.
  • Technical information: the limited security and delivery logs created by our hosting and database providers.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, provide your private workspace, synchronize your records across devices, secure the service, and respond to support requests. Google account information is used only for sign-in and account identification.

Sharing and service providers

hibi does not sell personal information. Data is processed by service providers needed to operate the app: Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and database hosting, and Cloudflare for website delivery and security. These providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Student and guardian information

Account holders decide what class records to enter and are responsible for having a lawful reason and any permission required to store them, especially information about minors. Do not enter information that is unnecessary for class management.

Retention, export, and deletion

You can export a JSON backup or clear your workspace from the app. To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, contact the address below from the email used for your account. Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Security and international processing

hibi uses account-scoped database controls and reputable infrastructure providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Depending on provider infrastructure, information may be processed outside your country.

Your choices

You may stop using hibi, export or clear workspace data, revoke hibi's Google access in your Google Account, or contact us about access, correction, or deletion requests.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Material updates will be reflected by a new effective date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hibicontact.old339@passinbox.com.
